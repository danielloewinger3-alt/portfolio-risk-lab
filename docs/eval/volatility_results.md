## Caveats

- Evaluation points are every 5 trading days but each target spans 21 days, so consecutive losses overlap; naive standard errors would be understated. QLIKE standard errors and every 'meaningful' statement use Newey-West with lag 21 (evaluation points), which is conservative.
- 'Meaningful' means a Newey-West t-statistic of the QLIKE loss differential of at least 1.96 in absolute value and a relative QLIKE gap of at least 1%. This is a Diebold-Mariano-style check, approximate under overlap.
- LightGBM predicts log realized volatility and the prediction is exponentiated with no bias correction (per spec), so it targets the conditional median; QLIKE penalizes under-forecasting, which works against it.
- Hyperparameters (LightGBM 200 trees, depth 3, learning rate 0.05, subsample 0.8; EWMA lambda 0.94; GARCH(1,1)-t) were fixed before any evaluation. Nothing was tuned on the test period.
- One 10-year window per portfolio ending at the data snapshot; results depend on the period (it includes the 2020 COVID shock and the 2022 rate shock).
